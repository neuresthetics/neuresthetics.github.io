Part one

RestoKit: the framework

RestoKit is the framework: the job stages (phases) and the modules that load into an AI model. This part of the page is about the kit itself: what it is, where it came from, and how it is built. The tools built on it have their own section further down.

How it changes the day

  • Edge cases stay in the kit. The rare ones don’t come up often enough to stay top of mind. You carry less of that inventory in your head.
  • The claim trail rides with the work. Photos, moisture, why the equipment is there and where it sits, and a short note happen on the stop.
  • One question at a time. The answer is short and specific, the way a senior tech talks the job through.

What it is

RestoKit is a structured JSON kit for anyone running a restoration job. It’s grounded in IICRC S500 (water) and S520 (mold) principles, and written the way a senior tech talks a job through.

It doesn’t replace years on the job. It carries lessons from people who’ve spent decades in the trade. Its main job is memory: the rare edge cases that don’t come up often enough to stay top of mind.

Who it’s for

  • Tech The job flow in order, with safety checks called out when they matter: category, electrical, crawlspace gas.
  • Lead Containment, negative air, equipment sizing, and crew and labor calls.
  • PM Hold status, work authorization, daily drying readings, and verification before teardown.
  • Estimator The Phase 0 survey and scope, remove-vs-dry and category calls, plus documentation that backs up the scope.

How it works

  1. Load it

    Attach resto_kit_0.9.20.json in the Grok app or add it to a Grok Bot’s knowledge. Nothing to install.

  2. Tell it the job

    For example: “Category 2 supply-line leak, kitchen and crawlspace, 1990s house, I’m the PM.”

  3. Ask one thing at a time

    “Remove or dry this LVP?” “Is the subfloor dry?”

  4. Get a field-ready answer

    Short and specific, grounded in IICRC S500 and S520 principles. Deeper detail only when you ask.

What’s inside

  • Five gated phases From Survey & Estimate to Closeout, so safety steps don’t get skipped. Hold is a status a job can enter from any phase.
  • Modules Category, remove vs. dry, containment, negative air, equipment sizing, mold, crawlspaces, hazard sampling, and more.
  • Base science RH, temperature, GPP, vapor drive, wicking, material behavior, dry standards.
  • Field lessons From daily job logs, added only once they prove out on real jobs.

Kit status

Kit version 0.9.20

  • 0.9.15. Morning into-file notes are delta-only: decisions, today’s work, and who’s assigned. Not a reprint of data already in the file.
  • 0.9.14. File review runs before the day-forward plan. To-dos come back role-sorted: PM, then Lead, then Techs.

Earlier 0.9.x stays in the kit. Next, it keeps growing as a field memory and voice guide, and keeps lining field scope up with estimating. For shops, a demo is on request. I can build a company version around a shop’s own procedures. The full kit is private; the public repo shows what it covers and how it’s laid out.

Development history

  • Pre-0.6. First single-file kit, then split into a modular kit.
  • 0.6.7 to 0.6.9. First numbered releases.
  • 0.8.x. Base for the 0.8 line, then field-memory expansion: crawlspaces rebuilt around displacement, combustion backdrafting, condition labels, cleaning stacks, dehumidifier ducting, and a job-type gate.
  • 0.9.0. Depth cap, four tiers, citations and notes moved out of the kit body, and a pump truck module.
  • 0.9.1. Job flow restructured into five phases, survey to closeout, plus Hold as a status. Hazard sampling rebuilt.
  • 0.9.3 to 0.9.5. Daily log template loads with the kit; job file review workflow and its checks.
  • 0.9.6 to 0.9.10. Cut clearance, moisture mapping tape line, day forward plan format, shower-over-crawl checks.
  • 0.9.11 to 0.9.13. Visit-note cadence, photo timing, and note style.
  • 0.9.14 and 0.9.15. File review before the day-forward plan, role-sorted; morning into-file notes stay delta-only.
  • 0.9.16 to 0.9.20. Technology, remote monitoring, job-site connectivity, scoping, and equipment notes.

Full history is in the public repo.

Part two

RestoKit tools: the showcase

RestoKit tools are the applications built on top of the framework: the ops board, Job Digest, ScopeBot and the rest. They follow the kit’s stages and rules; they are not the kit itself.

RestoKit tools: feature showroom

These are the board's tools as they look today, one screenshot each, running on demo data. Every name, job number and address is made up. The board ties into the RestoKit kit (the rules), the tower API (serves board.json), read-only Encircle digs (job data), the board manual and scope_bot. Tap any shot to enlarge.

Main page, all sections collapsed

The board's front page. One Ops panel with a collapsible section per tool; each header shows a live count or status (jobs on board, trucks staffed, stops today).

  • Collapse all / Expand all
  • Sync stamp and job count in the header
  • Desktop and phone layouts

Ties in: Reads board.json, which the tower host rebuilds from read-only Encircle digs.

Personnel

Who is on the crew today, their role and the truck they're in.

  • Role and default vehicle per person
  • Tap a name for skills, licenses and notes
  • Local-only edits, never synced

Ties in: Feeds Vehicles and Dispatch so crews are suggested from people who are actually available.

Vehicles

Seats people into trucks for the day.

  • Drag or tap to seat a person
  • Crew suggestions from today's jobs, two-person jobs first
  • Return to defaults / set defaults

Ties in: Uses Personnel plus the job board's due stops; the seats drive Dispatch and the Labor ratio.

Job Digest

A job-by-job briefing so office and field start from the same picture. Shown collapsed here; the header chip names the three fields.

  • Get in: what came in on the job
  • State: where the job stands now
  • Next: the next action

Ties in: Same jobs as the job board, folded into one short briefing per job.

Job board

Every open job as a card in the lane for its stage, from call to close.

  • Lanes (Call/Inspect, Work Authorization, Hazard testing, Abatement, Demo, Dry out, On hold, Close) or table view
  • Needs-action filters: missing work auth, asbestos/lead not negative, lockbox and spider placement
  • Locked by default; unlock to edit

Ties in: Lanes follow the kit's phase sequence. Card data comes from the Encircle digs through the tower API.

Labor ratio

Due stops divided by the stops the crew can make today, as a plain call: OK, busy or too busy.

  • Two-person vs solo check coverage
  • Stuck jobs by lane
  • Short explainer of what the ratio measures

Ties in: Built from the job board and today's Vehicles seats.

Triage

An intake script for the office on a new loss call, most urgent first.

  • Tap what the caller says; the board suggests urgency
  • Only asks what a caller can answer; the tech measures the rest
  • Writes are disabled when offline (as in this demo)

Ties in: Questions and urgency rules come from the kit's intake guidance; a filled intake becomes a new job card.

Dispatch

Builds the day's routes from the trucks going out and the stops due.

  • Suggest crews from jobs, compute routes, clear
  • Per-device settings for crews and special conditions
  • Optional resto_kit_bot review of the plan

Ties in: Uses Vehicles, the job board and the Map; route order shows as numbered pins.

Map

Job pins on a map, colored by lane.

  • Lane filters, fit to jobs
  • Show today's routes with truck stop order
  • Rings mark jobs that need a stop today

Ties in: Pins come from the job board; routes come from Dispatch. (Demo data here has no pins placed.)

Hazard survey

Jobs that still need asbestos or lead testing.

  • Pending / unknown / positive status per job
  • Close and On hold skipped
  • Mark a request locally with Update

Ties in: Read-only from board.json; results gate Demo per the kit's hazard rules.

ScopeBot

The billable line-item list ScopeBot scopes against, in order of work.

  • 10 categories, 114 items
  • Cat 3 flags on items that apply to Category 3 losses
  • No prices

Ties in: scope_bot reads job notes and photos and maps findings to these items; codes show here once it has run.

Templates

Blank note templates for copy and paste into the job file.

  • Inspection, every-visit, scope/rooms, plan and holds
  • One-tap Copy
  • No job data in the templates

Ties in: Matches the kit's note structure, so notes land in the file the way the file-quality check expects.

PM analysis

A project manager's view of what has stalled.

  • Stalled jobs (5+ days idle), oldest first
  • Jobs past inspection with no work authorization
  • On hold and open counts

Ties in: Computed from the digs' last-activity dates and the job board lanes.

File quality

Scores each active job file and lists what's missing.

  • Health score and fix-these-first list
  • Checks photos per room, notes per visit and recency
  • Rolling 7-day window

Ties in: Metrics follow the kit's file-quality chapter; data is read-only from Encircle digs.

Features

A searchable list of every board feature and the reason it exists.

  • 57 features grouped by tool
  • Each one says why: a kit rule or an ops rule
  • Search box

Ties in: Ties each feature back to the kit version it follows (references hidden in this shot).

Dev

Developer notes and a systems check.

  • How the board reads Encircle: data flow, write policy, PII handling, refresh cadence
  • Version history
  • Live systems check of the tower host

Ties in: Documents the tower API and the read-only Encircle digs. Section bodies are collapsed here.

Manual / Lookup

The board manual: searchable chapters with quick-lookup chips.

  • Chapters and sections with expand/collapse
  • Quick chips: moisture readings, containment, asbestos, PPE, photos and more
  • Cross-links into board tools

Ties in: A field reference that follows the kit; only chapter headings are shown here.
