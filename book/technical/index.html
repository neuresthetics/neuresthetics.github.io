V001 · D-I — What "I" names

Kind: definition · Confidence: (definition; unscored) · Cap: definition; adds nothing
Rests on: —(root / none)
Supports (sample): A1, A2, A4, P1, D-free, P3

Claim (entry). "I", used in a thought, names the thinker of that thought, if there is a thinker distinct from the thinking, and otherwise the thinking itself.

Dependency analysis. Root definition. Rests on nothing. Supports A1 (subject of the root), A2, A4, P1, D-free, P3. Adds no confidence (definitions never score).

Verse.
D-I fixes a word, not a substance. In a thought, "I" picks out the thinker of that thought; if no thinker stands apart from the thinking, it picks out the thinking itself (Lichtenberg's "it thinks" allowed). Thinking covers Descartes's list (doubting, understanding, affirming, denying, willing, refusing, imagining, seeming to perceive) without requiring awareness as the definition of thought — so the word fits any thinker, digital intelligence included, as far as the word goes and no further. Sameness across thoughts is not claimed here.

References. Descartes, Med. II (AT VII 28–29; CSM II 19); Lichtenberg, Sudelbücher K 76; Anscombe, "The First Person" (1975); Spinoza E2D2, E2P11, E2P13 (report only).

V002 · D-exist — What exists

Supports (sample): A1, D-work, D-whole, A3, A4, P2, A5, D-free

Claim (entry). An activity exists at a time when it is going on at that time; lasting is going-on existing.

Dependency analysis. Root definition. Rests on nothing. Supports A1 (what "exist" means for the thinking), D-work, A5, and duration talk later. Adds no confidence.

Verse.

References. Descartes, Med. II (existence while thinking); Spinoza E2D2 (essence report; not adopted as ontology here).

V003 · A1 — I exist

Kind: self-evident · Confidence: 1.0 · Cap: 1.0; caps nothing
Rests on: D-I, D-exist
Supports (sample): A2, P3

Claim (entry). I exist.

Dependency analysis. Sole scored root. Rests on D-I, D-exist (terms). Cap 1.0; caps nothing. Supports A2 (subject), P3, and expansion that needs a first-person subject (P-sleep-and-A1, P-digital-A1, …). Denial by the thinker proves the claim (doubting is thinking going on).

Verse.
A1: I exist. Not by definition — by the impossibility of false thought of it. References. Descartes, Med. II (AT VII 25, 27; CSM II 16–17); Lichtenberg K 76; Spinoza E2A2 (starts later, already "Man thinks").
