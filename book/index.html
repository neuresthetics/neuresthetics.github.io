Freedom of Necessity

bookaxiom by axiom

Working title: Freedom of Necessity: God, Brain, and the Order of the Mind. A book written axiom by axiom, in the geometric style of Spinoza's Ethics.

It's part of the Neuresthetics research, which goes back to before AI tools existed.

How it's written

Each entry is a definition, axiom, or proposition, and cites the entries it builds on. The first axiom is "I, a human, exist."

id: A1
kind: axiom
text: "I, a human, exist."  # the first axiom

How it's checked

The repo also holds a harness. A local open-weights model checks each entry against what it cites, and no entry can be more confident than its weakest support.

Status

The entries are unscored right now while a new checking step is built.
