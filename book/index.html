Freedom of Necessity

bookaxiom by axiom

Working title: Freedom of Necessity: God, Brain, and the Order of the Mind. A book written axiom by axiom, in the geometric order of Spinoza's Ethics. It starts from "I exist" and argues that there is one order, Nature, and that the mind is the body's own organization described from within. Freedom isn't escape from causes. It's knowing where a cause comes from and acting from that knowledge. It's part of the Neuresthetics research.

Spinoza's own manuscript of the Ethics is lost; only copies survive. This is an attempt to run his method again from first principles, with digital intelligence: the author sets the starting point and the rules, and Grok writes and checks each step.

Start here

The preface, On a Book Without Its Original, says what the book takes from Spinoza and what it adds.

The quickest way in is the current rough draft, Axioms of Necessity v12. It's the whole book in 174 short verses, derived from core v2, that you can open at any page.

53. There is one order, and it has no outside. Whatever is, is in it. Call it God, or Nature.

143. Freedom doesn't need that you could have done otherwise, given all that came before. It needs that what you did came from you.

86. An explanation is a lamp, not a proof. It shows where to look, and it tests what is offered as evidence, though never the evidence for itself; only evidence that holds up settles what you find.

How it's written

Each entry is a definition, axiom, or proposition, and cites the entries it builds on. Every entry is tagged by kind (true by definition, backed by evidence, or a model) so philosophy can't pass itself off as science. The arc starts from the barest claim: an I exists, finds it is a body, finds the body is a whole, and finds it is a member of something larger.

How it's checked

A claim and its negation are each argued at full strength, and only what survives on checked evidence is kept. Before a run, each side has to say what result would prove it wrong. No entry can be more confident than its weakest support. The book is written and checked with Grok. There are no harness scores yet.
