🧠 Neuresthetics

Jason Burns · 🌐 neuresthetic.net

github · neurestheticsbuilder · tools + researchsince 2017

Neuresthetics is kinesthetics for brains: spelled with "eu," never "neuroesthetics." Plural, it's the practice: shaping how a mind is organized, on purpose. Singular, a neuresthetic is a result of that practice.

I build tools for people who work with their hands and their heads: 🛠️ a restoration kit for water, mold, and crawlspace work, 🗣️ a word board for people who tap words instead of speaking, and 📜 long-running research on how minds put order on the world. Neuresthetics started before AI. AI is one of the tools now, not the point.

⚙️ HOW THE WORK IS CHECKED

One rule runs through every project below.

The rule. A claim stands only if its source says so, word for word. Models draft, search and argue; code and blind audit runs check what they produce, and anything unchecked is labeled a draft. Arguments are checked against fallacy_catalog (v0.6.1), a sourced catalog of 221 named fallacies with a method for checking one step at a time, built to avoid false accusations. A flag is a lead to check, not a verdict. Models are tools here, not the point.

Local models. A Linux tower with a 20 GB GPU and 64 GB RAM runs Ollama with Qwen 27B, in a standard and an uncensored build, at 32K context (argument runs use 16K). A stock 14B has already run as the swap model. The tower works through unattended job queues, for days if needed, for an adversarial argument harness: the models draft the strongest case for each side, code checks every cite against word-for-word source excerpts, and runs are rescored and repeated across seeds and model swaps to see how much of a result comes from the model. It has no valid scores yet.

Grok. Grok Bot (an xAI assistant) runs a set of project bots for coding, audits, writing support, and fetching the word-for-word sources those cite checks use.

🛠️ "REAL WORLD" PRODUCTS

Made to be useful to other people.

💧 RestoKit

Kit 0.9.15. A restoration kit for water, mold, and crawlspace work, from tech to lead, PM, and estimator. Grounded in IICRC S500 and S520 principles, it walks a job through five phases from survey to closeout, with Hold as a status from any phase, and keeps the rare edge cases. The full kit is private.

⚙️ Runs on: commercial models only, loaded into the Grok app or a Grok Bot

🗣️ ANOVA Language

A free word board for people who communicate by tapping words instead of speaking. It's a web app for iPad and other tablets, and it works offline once it's installed. Buttons never move as the word levels grow. Version 1.4.1. MIT license.

⚙️ Runs on: No model · plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, offline, no network calls

🌐 page📂 repo

🔎 Grokipedia Audit

Checks Grokipedia articles against the sources they cite, one claim at a time, with the evidence kept next to every finding. Current results: a circumcision fallacy_catalog benchmark (58 articles) and a Spinoza citation audit (401 sentences).

⚙️ Runs on: A general-purpose AI agent · plain written steps and fallacy_catalog; each finding records the model and date

🧠 BRAND PROJECT

The Neuresthetics project itself: the study and the book.

The Genius Study rebuilds, from sources, where remembered geniuses stand on God, nature and law. Drafts only; no results yet. Freedom of Necessity is the geometric book that starts from "I exist." Its current face is one chain, technical and poetic (V001–V251).

🔬 Genius Study

The study. Where the people history remembers as geniuses stand on God, nature and law. The public record is rebuilt from sources, one person at a time, beside a labeled belief that learning lawful order early may pay off. Drafts only; no results yet.

⚙️ Runs on: Grok · one Grok Bot drafts person records from web search and the cited pages; blind audit runs check them (unreviewed drafts)

📖 Freedom of Necessity

The book. Written axiom by axiom from "I exist," in the geometric order of Spinoza's Ethics. One order, Nature; the mind is the body's own organization described from within. His manuscript is lost; this runs the method again from first principles. Current face: B1 dual render, technical and poetic, V001–V251.

⚙️ Runs on: Grok · written and checked with Grok; core v3 dual render is the readable face, and core v2 is still the last seed in entries/; no harness scores yet beyond A1 (scored 1.0)
