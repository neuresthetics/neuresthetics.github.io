🧠 Neuresthetics

Jason Burns · 🌐 neuresthetic.net

github · neurestheticsbuilder · tools + researchsince 2017

Neuresthetics is kinesthetics for brains: spelled with "eu," never "neuroesthetics." Plural, it's the practice: shaping how a mind is organized, on purpose. Singular, a neuresthetic is a result of that practice.

I build tools for people who work with their hands and their heads: 🛠️ a restoration kit for water, mold, and crawlspace work, 🗣️ a free offline word board for people who tap words instead of speaking, and 📜 long-running research on how minds put order on the world. Neuresthetics started before AI. AI is one of the tools now, not the point.

🛠️ "REAL WORLD" PRODUCTS

Made to be useful to other people.

💧 RestoKit

Kit 0.9.20. A restoration kit for water, mold, and crawlspace work, from tech to lead, PM, and estimator. Grounded in IICRC S500 and S520 principles; five phases from survey to closeout, with Hold from any phase. Recent kit work covers walkthrough note sets and visit notes that stack newest day first. The public page also shows the RestoKit tools showcase (ops board, Job Digest, ScopeBot, and related demos on synthetic data). The full kit is private.

⚙️ Runs on: commercial models only, loaded into the Grok app or a Grok Bot

🗣️ ANOVA Language

A free, offline AAC word board for iPad and other tablets. Buttons never move as word levels grow. Version 1.6.0: Paths for short grammatical sentences, age-tiered levels (Starter through Full), and button looks (classic, chunky, tint, pebble). MIT license. Open the live board from the project page; no shared account needed.

⚙️ Runs on: No model · plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, offline, no network calls

🔎 Grokipedia Audit

Checks Grokipedia articles against the sources they cite, one claim at a time, with the evidence kept next to every finding. Current published results: a circumcision and FGM benchmark (58 articles) and a Spinoza citation audit (401 sentences). In progress: automated idea_detection dry runs on 2,616 circumcision argument passages (leads, not verdicts). Consciousness pages are snapshotted only; no checks on that set yet.

⚙️ Runs on: A general-purpose AI agent · plain written steps; each finding records the model and date

📖 Freedom of Necessity

The book. Freedom of Necessity is a Spinoza-style geometric work — definitions, axioms, and propositions in one chain from the root axiom A1, “I exist.” Live draft is core v3 (BASE 20 + expansion 231 = 251) with dual verse tracks, poetic and technical, sharing the same V001–V251 numbers. The collision record is the book.

⚙️ Runs on: Grok · core v3 dual-track draft; harness scores dropped from the process (2026-11-09); no results to announce
