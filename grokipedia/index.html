Grokipedia Audit

auditcitation check · fallacy_catalog

Checks Grokipedia articles against the sources they cite, one claim at a time, with the evidence kept next to every finding so a reader can check the work without having to trust it.

What it is

Grokipedia is xAI's AI-written encyclopedia. Its articles look well sourced, but an AI writer can attach a citation that doesn't say what the sentence claims, or let the numbering slip so a footnote points to the wrong source. This audit looks for those problems claim by claim.

Who it's for

Readers and editors of Grokipedia, people studying AI-written reference text, and anyone who would rather check a finding than take it on trust.

How it works

A general-purpose AI agent follows plain written steps. No custom framework, scoring system or private vocabulary.

  • Snapshot. Save the article as it appeared on the day of the audit, because Grokipedia pages change.
  • Claim and source. Quote what the article says, then quote what the cited source actually says.
  • Verdict. Mark each claim supported, miscited (the source exists but doesn't say this), unsupported (the source contradicts it or gives nothing to check) or uncited. Article, source and judgment are kept separate.
  • Reasoning check. Where an article argues rather than reports, the argument is checked against fallacy_catalog, a plain, sourced list of known logical fallacies. A flag is a lead to follow up, not a verdict.

Every finding records the date and the model that made the judgment. Nothing is presented as a human expert's ruling, and citations or quotes are never filled in from memory.
