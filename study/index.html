Genius Study

studyv8 in progress

A study of where remembered genius sits on a scale of lawful, non-intervening order, plus a labeled belief model of how learning that order early might pay off.

What it is

One home for every version of the Neuresthetics study. It has two lanes. Lane A describes what the public record shows about each person. Lane B states a belief out loud and writes it so it can fail. Lane A doesn't depend on Lane B.

The roster of 1,380 people comes from five LLM-generated lists; 441 of them are core candidates.

How it works

  • One person per run. Each run picks the next person, researches them from primary and scholarly sources, fills their record (leaving TODO where nothing is sourced yet), validates it and commits.
  • Belief systems are extended the same way, one record at a time.
  • Checked by script. Schemas and validators check every record, and the roster rebuilds from the raw lists.
  • Audited. Claim packets from the records go through blind substance_lens runs, and the fixes are logged in an audit report.

Status

Version 8 rebuilds the data from the original model lists. v8.0-alpha is tagged as a GitHub prerelease: scaffolding and draft records only. The latest complete release is v7.1.
